How this works

In the interest of transparency: this is not an AI model reading your photograph. Here is exactly what happens.

01

A short questionnaire

Three questions about your hair length, hair texture, and the style you're aiming for. This is the primary input.

02

An optional photo check

Your browser measures your photo's brightness, sharpness, and resolution directly — genuine calculation, performed on your device.

03

A matched consultation

Your answers are matched against a curated set of written consultations. This is the closest available match, not a bespoke reading of your photo.

04

Your photo's real findings, included

If your photo was dim or unclear, that observation is genuinely drawn from your image and included in your notes.
